What should you check before buying an air fryer in India?

Four things decide whether an air fryer earns its counter space: capacity (litres), wattage, basket type, and cleaning ease. For an Indian family of 3-4, 4-5 litres is the practical minimum - smaller fryers fit a handful of fries but not a full meal. Wattage matters less than the brand's heating design: a 1200W fryer from a good brand can beat a 1500W from a weak one. And because air fryers need cleaning after most uses, a dishwasher-safe, non-stick basket is worth paying for.

Also check the actual basket dimensions, not just litre claims - a tall narrow 4L basket fits fewer chips than a wide 4L one. Look at review photos of the basket before buying.

How do you use an air fryer properly?

  1. Preheat for 3-5 minutes - most models have a preheat function; it makes a real difference to crispness.
  2. Do not overcrowd the basket. Food needs airflow; cook in single layers or shake halfway through.
  3. Shake or flip food at the halfway mark for even browning.
  4. Lightly coat food in oil (1 teaspoon is usually enough) - "oil-free" is a marketing term, a little oil makes things crisp.
  5. Clean the basket after each use - food residue burns on and ruins the non-stick coating over time.

Should you buy a small or large air fryer?

For 1-2 people, a 4L fryer is fine but the basket fills fast. For 3+ people, go 4.5L or larger - the Philips NA120 (4.2L) is borderline, while the Ninja MAX PRO (6.2L) handles a full family meal. A larger fryer also cooks more evenly because food has more airflow room. The counter space cost is the only downside.

How much electricity does an air fryer use?

About 1.2-1.5 kWh per hour of use (typical of 1400-1500W fryers running at full power, per manufacturer spec sheets) - similar to a microwave. A 20-minute air-fry session costs roughly ₹5-8 at typical Indian tariffs - cheaper than a gas oven and faster. Your actual draw depends on wattage and how often the element cycles.

Can you cook Indian food in an air fryer?

Yes - samosas, pakoras, tawa-style paneer, roasted chana, and even reheating parathas work well. It will not replace a kadhai for deep-frying, but it handles most "crispy" cravings with far less oil.

How long do air fryers last?

Good brands (Philips, Ninja, COSORI) typically last 3-5 years with regular use, based on warranty periods and user review patterns. The non-stick basket coating is the first thing to wear; replacing the basket (where available) extends the life.
