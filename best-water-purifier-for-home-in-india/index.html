What type of water purifier does your home need?

The right purifier depends on your water source, not your budget. Municipal (tap) water needs at least RO+UV or RO+UV+UF. Borewell water - common in apartments and many cities - almost always needs RO because of high TDS (dissolved solids). If your TDS is above 500 ppm, a plain UV purifier will not fix the taste or hardness; you need RO. If your water is already low-TDS (some city supplies), a UV+UF purifier is cheaper and wastes less water.

Two more factors: water wastage (RO purifiers reject 20-50% of water - newer "water saver" models reduce this) and service availability. A purifier is a service-heavy appliance; buying a brand with local service (Aquaguard/ESSELL, KENT, Pureit) matters more than a slightly lower price from an unknown brand.

Is KENT Grand worth the premium?

KENT Grand (₹11,899, 4.3 stars) is the premium default - KENT is the best-known purifier brand in India, with the deepest service presence, and their RO+UF+TDS control technology is proven. You pay a KENT premium over functionally similar Aquaguard/Pureit models, but you buy certainty: parts, service, and resale value. For a long-term appliance used daily by the whole family, that certainty has real value.

How do you choose between RO, UV, and UF?

  1. High TDS (borewell/hard water, over 500 ppm): RO, or RO+UV if you also want germ protection.
  2. Low TDS municipal water: UV+UF is enough and wastes no water.
  3. Uncertain source: RO+UV+UF is the complete package.
  4. Check your TDS first - a ₹300 TDS meter tells you which category you need before spending ₹8,000-15,000.

Should you worry about RO water wastage?

Standard RO purifiers reject 20-50% of input water, which matters where water is scarce or metered. Models like the Pureit Eco Water Saver (₹10,999) recover more water. If water availability is a concern in your area, prioritise a water-saver model - the higher price pays for itself in reduced wastage over years.

FAQ

Which water purifier is best for home use in India?

Aquaguard Sure Delight NXT RO+UV (₹7,999, 4.3 stars, free service) or Pureit Wave Plus (₹7,799, 4.4 stars) - both trusted brands with strong service networks at a sensible price.

Is RO necessary for purifying water in India?

For borewell or high-TDS water, yes - UV alone cannot remove dissolved solids. For low-TDS municipal water, UV+UF is sufficient and wastes no water.

Which brand is best for water purifiers in India?

KENT, Aquaguard (ESSELL), and Pureit have the strongest service networks. For most homes, Aquaguard or Pureit at the ₹8,000 price point beats a more expensive KENT of similar spec.

How often should a water purifier be serviced?

Every 6-12 months, depending on water quality. Filters need replacement (RO membrane roughly every 1-2 years, pre/post filters more often). This is why service availability matters when choosing a brand.

What is a good budget for a home RO purifier?

₹7,000-12,000 covers trusted-brand RO+UV purifiers (Aquaguard, Pureit, KENT entry models). Below ₹6,000 you are in unknown-brand territory with thinner service support.

Are copper or alkaline purifiers worth the extra cost?

Models like the Livpure Allura Prime (₹11,499) add a copper or alkaline cartridge on top of RO+UV+UF. The copper/alkaline health claims are mostly marketing - the RO+UV+UF core is what actually cleans the water. Pay for copper only if the rest of the spec is what you need anyway; do not let the cartridge be the reason you buy.

Should I buy a purifier with a service plan?

If the plan is included free (like the Aquaguard's ₹2,000 plan), it is a real saving because a purifier needs a service visit every 6-12 months and filter replacements. If you are paying extra for the plan, compare it against your local service prices first - an annual ₹1,000-1,500 service cost is normal for trusted brands.
