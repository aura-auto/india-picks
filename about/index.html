About

India Picks publishes research-backed guides on honest product guides for indian shoppers. Every guide starts from primary sources — official pricing pages, spec sheets, and live product listings — and nothing gets published until the load-bearing facts are verified.

How we research

  • Prices and specs are checked against official sources before publishing
  • We update guides as products and prices change
  • We disclose clearly when we may earn a commission
  • If we cannot verify a fact, we say so instead of guessing

Who writes this

India Picks is run by a small independent editorial team (Aura Editorial). We are not affiliated with the brands we cover beyond standard affiliate relationships, which are always disclosed.

Corrections

Spotted an error? Write to us via the contact page and we will correct it promptly.
