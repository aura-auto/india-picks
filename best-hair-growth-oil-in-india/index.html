What actually works in hair growth oils?

Be honest about the evidence: no oil grows hair like a prescription treatment, but two ingredients have real research behind them. Rosemary oil has clinical trials showing hair growth comparable to minoxidil 2% over 6 months. Bhringraj, amla, and hibiscus (the Ayurvedic staples) have traditional use and some supporting studies for scalp health and reduced breakage, though the evidence is weaker. Everything else in a hair oil - marketing claims about "instant growth" - is cosmetic.

The realistic role of hair oil: it improves scalp condition, reduces breakage (which makes hair *appear* to grow faster), and keeps hair healthier so it retains length. That is worth doing, but set expectations - you are not going to regrow a receding hairline with ₹250 oil. If hair fall is severe or sudden, see a dermatologist; oils are maintenance, not treatment.

Do onion oils work for hair fall?

Onion juice has small studies suggesting it may help patchy hair loss, which is why Mamaearth's Onion oil (₹249, 4.0 stars) and Sacred Grove's Rosemary+Onion (₹722, 4.4 stars) sell well. The evidence is thinner than rosemary's and the onion smell is intense. They are worth trying alongside a rosemary or Ayurvedic oil, not instead of one.

How should you apply hair oil correctly?

  1. Warm 3-5 drops of oil between your palms (do not microwave it).
  2. Massage into the scalp with fingertips in small circles for 5-10 minutes - massage itself improves scalp circulation.
  3. Apply from roots to tips for the remaining oil; focus on dry ends.
  4. Leave it in for 30-60 minutes, or overnight if your scalp tolerates it.
  5. Wash with a gentle shampoo. Oiling 2-3 times a week is enough; daily oiling can clog follicles.

How long until you see results from hair oil?

Hair grows about 1-1.25 cm per month regardless of what you apply. Oil helps by reducing breakage and improving scalp health, so visible improvement in thickness and length retention typically takes 3-6 months of consistent use. Any product promising visible results in weeks is overstating. Take a photo at month zero and compare at month three - that is the honest way to judge.

FAQ

Which hair oil is best for hair growth in India?

Himalaya Ayurveda Secrets (₹381, 4.9 stars) is the best-rated; Parachute Advansed Rosemary (₹243, 4.2 stars) is the evidence-backed pick. Both are safe daily-use options.

Does rosemary oil really grow hair?

Clinical trials show rosemary oil can be comparable to minoxidil 2% for androgenetic hair loss over 6 months. It needs consistent daily application - it is not a quick fix.

Can hair oil regrow hair on a bald patch?

Unlikely. Oil improves scalp health and reduces breakage but cannot regrow hair where follicles have permanently miniaturised. See a dermatologist for pattern baldness; minoxidil or finasteride are the treatments with evidence.

How often should you oil your hair?

2-3 times a week is the sweet spot for most Indian hair types. Daily oiling can oversaturate the scalp and attract dust, especially in urban areas.

Is coconut oil or onion oil better for hair?

For reducing protein loss and breakage, coconut oil has the strongest evidence. For potential hair-fall reduction, onion and rosemary have the studies. They address different problems - many people rotate between them.
