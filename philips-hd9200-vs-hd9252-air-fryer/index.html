The short version of my conclusion: these are the same cooking machine underneath, and Philips themselves market both as the 3000 Series Airfryer. Are the Philips HD9200 and HD9252 actually different?

Yes, but only in two meaningful ways. According to the official philips.co.in spec sheets (fetched 14 August 2026), everything else matches almost line for line:

  • Control interface: HD9252/90 is Digital (touch screen). HD9200/90 is Analogue (a manual dial).
  • Keep-warm function: HD9252/90 has it (Yes, keeps food at temperature for up to 30 minutes). HD9200/90 does not (No).
  • Weight: HD9252 is 4.55 kg; HD9200 is 4.5 kg. A non-factor.

Everything else on the official spec sheets is identical: 4.1L capacity, 1400W power, Rapid Air technology, maximum temperature 200°C, 7 presets, adjustable timer up to 60 minutes, automatic shut-off, removable and dishwasher-safe basket, cool-touch hand grips, cool-wall enclosure, single basket, no connectivity, dimensions 360 x 264 x 295 mm, and a 2-year warranty.

So the honest answer is: the cooking performance and capacity are effectively the same. The difference is how you control it and whether you get keep-warm.

Which one should I buy - the HD9200 or the HD9252?

Start with the price gap, because that is the single biggest variable in this decision. Neither model number has a fixed street price that holds across sellers, and Tata CLiQ re-prices regularly. A 4.1L Philips Rapid Air air fryer in this 3000-series line typically lists in the roughly Rs 8,000 to Rs 10,000 band in India, but the live price on Tata CLiQ on any given day is what actually matters. Compare the two listings side by side before deciding.

When to pick the HD9252/90: take the HD9252/90 if the premium over the HD9200 is small (a few hundred to around a thousand rupees), or if you value the touch screen and keep-warm enough to pay for them. The touch screen makes navigating the 7 presets and setting temperature a little more pleasant than spinning a dial, and keep-warm genuinely helps when you cook for a family and everyone eats at slightly different times. It is the more complete machine and the one I would default to.

When to pick the HD9200/90: choose the HD9200/90 when it is noticeably cheaper on the day you buy (a difference of a couple of thousand rupees or more), or when you simply prefer a physical dial. Some people find a rotary knob more reliable and less fiddly than a touch panel, and an analogue interface is one less electronic part to fail. For a strict budget buy where keeping the price down beats getting keep-warm, the HD9200 is the right call.

Avoid the HD9252 if: you are paying a big premium for features you will not use, and you would rather put the money toward a bigger fryer or accessories. The keep-warm button on a single-serve or couple household gets used far less than you would expect.

Avoid the HD9200 if: you plan to cook for a family where the last plate rarely matches the first, or you want the cleaner preset navigation of a touch screen and the small premium is acceptable.

FAQ

Is the Philips HD9252 better than the HD9200?

In hardware terms, yes, slightly. The HD9252/90 adds a digital touch screen and a keep-warm function (up to 30 minutes) that the HD9200/90 does not have. Capacity (4.1L), power (1400W), presets (7), max temperature (200°C) and warranty (2 years) are identical on the official philips.co.in spec sheets. Whether the HD9252 is "better" for you depends on how much you value keep-warm and touch control versus the price gap.

What is the difference between Philips HD9200 and HD9252 air fryers?

The HD9252/90 has a digital touch-screen interface and a keep-warm mode. The HD9200/90 has an analogue dial control and no keep-warm mode. That is the entire meaningful difference; every other spec on the official sheets matches (4.1L, 1400W, 200°C max, 7 presets, 2-year warranty, 360 x 264 x 295 mm dimensions).

Does the Philips HD9200 have keep warm?

No. The official philips.co.in spec sheet for the HD9200/90 lists the keep-warm function as "No." Keep-warm is exclusive to the HD9252/90 in this pair.

What is the warranty on the Philips HD9200 and HD9252?

Both carry a 2-year warranty according to the official philips.co.in product specifications. Always confirm the warranty terms on the listing at the time of purchase, since seller-specific extras are sometimes added.

Which Philips air fryer is cheaper, the HD9200 or HD9252?

Usually the HD9200/90, since it is the older, simpler model with fewer features. But Indian retail prices for both move frequently with sales, so the cheaper one on any given day can flip. Check the live Tata CLiQ listings for both before purchasing rather than assuming one is permanently cheaper.
